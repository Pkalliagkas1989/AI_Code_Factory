# Business brief: ai-code-factory

Updated: 2026-11-08. Status: provisional public example, not customer validation.

- Product: Claude Code skill and agents for spec → build → review → human merge.
  Sources: repository `README.md`, `.claude/skills/factory/SKILL.md`,
  `.claude/agents/approver.md` at base commit 2851b99.
- Stage/evidence: repository and recorded todo-app example jobs exist. They
  illustrate the workflow; they do not establish customer ROI, reliability at
  scale, security certification or guaranteed production readiness.
- Audience hypothesis: developers already using Claude Code who want a
  reviewable workflow and human control. No customer interviews supplied.
- Offer draft: try the workflow on one small, reversible feature and inspect
  its spec, review files and decision. Installation prerequisites are in README.
- Differentiation candidate: explicit review artifacts and human merge control;
  not a claim of superiority over competitors.
- Price, business model, paid channels, spend budget and conversion baseline:
  unknown. Do not invent them or imply a paid service/guarantee.
- Competitors: not researched for this brief; no comparative claims approved.
- Goal proposal: learn whether a developer can complete a useful first job and
  understand the review report. No observed campaign results supplied.
- Permissions: draft and recommend only. Human approves public business copy,
  customer contact, pricing and spend.
- Next discovery: observe a consenting developer's first job; ask what was
  unclear, which review evidence was useful and whether they would repeat it.
