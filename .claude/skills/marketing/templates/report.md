## Usable deliverable

## Evidence and alternatives

For each material recommendation: customer evidence; publication ID, finding,
setting, access basis and limitation; why it may transfer; a credible competing
approach including no action. State when a principle does not apply.

## Measurement and next action

Use the experiment fields: primary outcome, comparison, audience, sampling
rationale, timeframe, budget/effort cap, customer/economic guardrails and decision
rules. For supplied results give original counts, rates, uncertainty, attribution
limits and continue/change/stop/inconclusive recommendation.

## Bibliography

List the actual sources used with author, year, title, venue and DOI/URL; record
abstract-only or unavailable access. Keep synthetic examples separate from real
observations. Link private evidence without exposing customer information.

## Human decisions and handoff

concrete action. In factory mode include proposed acceptance criteria for the
spec writer; do not authorize code, publishing, spend or merge.
